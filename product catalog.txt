# Replace Synthetic Product Catalog with Real Blinkit Public Catalog

The current product catalog contains unrealistic and fabricated products such as incorrect brand-product combinations (e.g., Britannia Face Wash, Dove Measuring Cups).

Do NOT generate synthetic products.

Instead, rebuild the entire product catalog using ONLY publicly available Blinkit product listings.

## Objective

Create a high-quality product_catalog.json containing real products that are currently or recently available on Blinkit's public platform.

## Data Collection Strategy

Collect product information from Blinkit's publicly accessible product pages and category listings.

If direct crawling is restricted, use publicly indexed Blinkit product pages that are accessible through search engines.

Do not invent products.

Do not invent brands.

Do not invent prices.

If a product cannot be verified from a public source, exclude it.

## Data Quality Rules

Every product must satisfy all of the following:

✓ Product name matches Blinkit's public listing.

✓ Brand is correct.

✓ Category is correct.

✓ Variant is correct.

✓ Size is correct.

✓ Pricing reflects the public listing.

✓ Image URL points to the actual product image when available.

✓ Product URL points to the public product page when available.

## Validation

Reject any product that appears to be AI-generated or cannot be verified from a public Blinkit listing.

Validate that:

• Britannia products are only food products.
• Dove products are only personal care products.
• Reynolds products are stationery products.
• Bananas are unbranded or sold under the correct produce brand.
• Brand-product relationships match real FMCG catalogues.

## Output

Generate a clean product_catalog.json containing approximately 500–1000 verified real Blinkit products.

Accuracy is more important than quantity.

The remaining datasets (customer profiles, purchase history, and current carts) should continue to use synthetic data that references these real products.

Do not fabricate any product information.
