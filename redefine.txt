Refine the existing Hybrid Recommendation Engine without changing its architecture or UI.

Keep the current pipeline:

Customer Persona
→ Purchase History
→ Current Cart
→ Candidate Generation
→ Rule-Based Scoring
→ Best Product Selection
→ LLM Explanation

Do NOT redesign the engine. Improve only the business logic.

### 1. Improve Candidate Generation

Replace completely random candidate selection with intelligent candidate generation.

Instead of selecting 15 random products from the catalogue, generate candidate products using:

• Customer Persona
• Favourite Categories
• Purchase History
• Current Cart
• Cross-category affinity mappings

Example:

Health & Wellness
→ Hydration
→ Healthy Snacks
→ Fresh Produce

Groceries
→ Snacks
→ Cooking Oils
→ Ready-to-Eat

Pet Care
→ Pet Grooming
→ Cleaning Essentials

Generate a relevant pool of 15 candidate products before scoring.

--------------------------------------------------

### 2. Replace Random Cart Compatibility

Remove the random +1 to +10 cart compatibility score.

Instead use deterministic product affinity rules.

Examples:

Milk
→ Cornflakes

Bread
→ Butter

Protein Powder
→ Electral

Oats
→ Dry Fruits

Rice
→ Pickles

Atta
→ Cooking Oil

Pet Food
→ Pet Shampoo

Dog Treats
→ Pet Toys

Cleaning Liquid
→ Air Freshener

Assign affinity scores based on these mappings rather than randomness.

--------------------------------------------------

### 3. Replace Random Base Score

Remove the random baseline score.

Instead use a fixed baseline score for all candidates.

Example:

Base Score = 10

This keeps recommendations deterministic and reproducible.

--------------------------------------------------

### 4. Improve Purchase History Scoring

Instead of giving every previously purchased category the same score, weight the score based on purchase frequency.

Example:

Frequently Purchased Category
+8

Occasionally Purchased
+5

Rarely Purchased
+2

--------------------------------------------------

### 5. Strengthen Persona Logic

Instead of relying only on keyword matching, create structured persona preferences.

Each persona should contain:

• Preferred Categories
• Preferred Product Types
• Lifestyle Preferences
• Shopping Intent

Example:

Fitness Enthusiast

Preferred Categories:
Health & Wellness
Fresh Produce
Protein Foods

Avoid:
Sugary Drinks
Processed Snacks

Family Shopper

Preferred Categories:
Groceries
Baby Care
Cleaning Essentials

Student

Preferred Categories:
Instant Food
Snacks
Soft Drinks

Pet Owner

Preferred Categories:
Pet Food
Pet Grooming
Cleaning Essentials

Use these structured preferences during scoring.

--------------------------------------------------

### 6. Preserve Existing Business Objective

Continue prioritising unexplored categories.

Category Novelty should remain the strongest scoring factor because the objective of this MVP is to increase cross-category discovery.

Do not recommend products from previously purchased categories unless their overall score is significantly higher than all unexplored category candidates.

--------------------------------------------------

### 7. Preserve Existing Explainability

Continue using the LLM ONLY after the rule engine has selected the winning product.

The LLM should never choose products.

It should only generate a concise 2–3 sentence explanation based on:

• Persona
• Purchase History
• Current Cart
• Selected Product
• Selected Category

--------------------------------------------------

The final system should remain deterministic, explainable, production-inspired, and closely aligned with Blinkit's business goal of increasing cross-category product discovery.
