Problem Statement: Blinkit Smart Discovery

Project Overview

Build an AI-native MVP called Blinkit Smart Discovery that helps Blinkit
users discover products from new categories through personalized,
context-aware recommendations during checkout.

Background

Research from public user feedback (Play Store, Reddit, forums) and user
surveys indicates: - Users repeatedly purchase the same categories. -
Shopping is highly mission-driven. - Users rarely browse unfamiliar
categories. - Personalized, explainable recommendations improve trust. -
One relevant recommendation is more effective than many generic
suggestions.

Business Goal

Increase the percentage of Monthly Active Customers (MACs) who purchase
from at least one new product category every month.

MVP Objective

Develop an AI-powered feature that: - Understands shopping intent. -
Detects repetitive buying patterns. - Identifies unexplored
categories. - Recommends one relevant product from a new category. -
Explains why the recommendation is relevant. - Lets users Add to Cart or
Skip.

Data Sources

1.  Real Blinkit Product Catalog (public product information)
2.  Synthetic Customer Profiles
3.  Synthetic Purchase History
4.  Synthetic Current Cart

AI Workflow

1.  Load product catalog.
2.  Retrieve customer profile.
3.  Analyze purchase history.
4.  Analyze current cart.
5.  Detect shopping intent.
6.  Calculate exploration score.
7.  Recommend one new category.
8.  Recommend one product.
9.  Generate a personalized explanation.
10. Display the recommendation during checkout.

Deliverable

A production-ready prototype demonstrating how AI can increase
cross-category purchases through personalized, explainable
recommendations integrated into the Blinkit shopping experience.
