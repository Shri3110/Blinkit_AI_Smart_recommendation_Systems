Reason

Instead of generating completely free-form AI text, use a template-driven explanation populated with dynamic customer data.

Template:

"You usually purchase products from **{Top Categories}**. **{Recommended Product}** complements your current cart and introduces you to the **{New Category}** category, which you haven't explored before. Customers with similar shopping behaviour frequently purchase this product together with similar orders."

Generate the values dynamically from:

• Selected Customer Persona

• Current Cart

• Purchase History

• Favourite Categories

• Previously Purchased Categories

• Product Affinity Rules

• Cross-category buying patterns

The recommendation should ALWAYS:

1. Match the customer's persona.

2. Complement the current cart.

3. Belong to a category the customer has never purchased before.

4. Be a realistic Blinkit product.

5. Be supported by simple rule-based logic before generating the explanation.

The AI explanation should NEVER:

❌ Mention unrelated concepts.

❌ Invent facts.

❌ Give health or lifestyle advice.

❌ Recommend products for illogical reasons.

❌ Produce long ChatGPT-style paragraphs.

Maximum explanation length: 2–3 sentences.
