Review and improve the recommendation engine business logic without changing the overall MVP architecture or UI.

Current issues observed:

1. Ensure recommended_category ALWAYS matches the selected product's actual category.
For example:
❌ recommended_category = "Dairy, Bread & Eggs"
Product = Britannia Good Day Butter Biscuits
Actual category = Biscuits & Bakery

This should never happen.

The backend must always set:

recommended_category = recommended_product.category

instead of calculating them separately.

------------------------------------------------

2. Improve recommendation explanations.

Do not use the same explanation for every user.

Generate explanations based on the user's persona and recommendation reason.

Examples:

Fitness Enthusiast:
"Based on your healthy shopping habits, this product is a great addition to your regular purchases while helping you explore a new category."

Parent:
"Parents with similar shopping patterns often purchase this product alongside their regular family essentials."

Working Professional:
"This product complements your routine purchases and introduces something new without changing your usual shopping behaviour."

Student:
"This quick and affordable product matches your shopping style while encouraging category exploration."

Premium Shopper:
"This premium recommendation aligns with your preferences while helping you discover another category."

------------------------------------------------

3. Improve matched_reasons.

Do not always return

- Matches your shopping preferences
- Helps you discover a new category

Instead generate dynamic reasons such as:

- Matches your healthy lifestyle
- Complements your breakfast purchases
- Popular among similar shoppers
- Frequently bought with your favourite products
- Fits your shopping routine
- Introduces a new category
- High affinity with your purchase history
- Relevant to your lifestyle
- Great complementary purchase

Return 2-3 reasons depending on the recommendation.

------------------------------------------------

4. Improve confidence score calculation.

Do not return mostly fixed values like 65 or 80.

Calculate confidence from recommendation signals.

Example:

Persona Match = +30
Purchase History = +25
Complementary Category = +20
Cross-category Novelty = +15
Product Availability = +10

Total = 100

Return the final confidence score only.

Typical range:
70–95

------------------------------------------------

5. Validate recommendation consistency before returning the response.

Before returning:

- recommended_category == recommended_product.category
- recommended_product exists
- explanation is not empty
- matched_reasons has at least two reasons
- confidence_score between 70 and 95
- is_new_category is correctly determined

------------------------------------------------

6. Improve synthetic product realism.

Avoid unrealistic combinations such as:

Amul Alphonso Mango
Amul Green Chilli

Brands should logically match products.

If necessary, correct the synthetic catalogue so product-brand combinations appear realistic.

------------------------------------------------

IMPORTANT

Do NOT redesign the application.

Do NOT modify the UI.

Do NOT change any API endpoints.

Do NOT change request/response schema.

Only improve the backend recommendation logic, explanations, confidence calculation, recommendation consistency, and synthetic catalogue quality while keeping the MVP behaviour exactly the same.
