Refine the Smart Discovery recommendation popup UI without changing the recommendation engine, scoring algorithm, business logic, or backend APIs.

The recommendation pipeline is complete. This iteration should focus only on improving clarity, trust, and user experience.

### 1. Remove the generic "CATEGORY" badge

Remove the generic "CATEGORY" pill.

Instead, display the actual product category with the existing "🟢 NEW CATEGORY" badge.

Example:

Dairy, Bread & Eggs   🟢 NEW CATEGORY

or

Category
Dairy, Bread & Eggs
🟢 NEW CATEGORY

The category name should be clear and readable.

--------------------------------------------------

### 2. Improve the AI Explanation

Keep the explanation concise (2–3 sentences).

The explanation should always reference the actual recommendation logic.

It should naturally combine:
• User persona
• Shopping behaviour
• Current cart (if applicable)
• Product relevance
• New category discovery (if applicable)

Avoid generic or repetitive phrases such as:
"We've found that..."

Instead, generate explanations similar to:

"Based on your fitness-focused shopping habits, Amul Curd complements your healthy purchases while introducing you to the Dairy, Bread & Eggs category that you haven't explored before."

or

"Since you frequently buy grocery essentials, Amul Curd is a natural addition to your cart and helps you discover a new category aligned with your shopping preferences."

The explanation should sound conversational, personalised, and directly related to the recommendation.

--------------------------------------------------

### 3. Display All Matching Reasons

Instead of showing only one reason, display every deterministic rule that contributed to the recommendation.

Examples:

Matched because

✓ Matches your shopping preferences

✓ Complements your current cart

✓ Based on your purchase history

✓ Frequently bought together

✓ Introduces a new category

Only display the reasons that actually contributed to the final score.

Do not display empty or irrelevant reasons.

--------------------------------------------------

### 4. Rename "AI Match Score"

Replace the label:

"AI Match Score"

with

"Recommendation Match"

or

"Recommendation Score"

This score is generated by deterministic business rules rather than AI confidence, so the terminology should better reflect the underlying logic.

Keep the percentage display exactly as it is.

Example:

Recommendation Match
84%
